Fixes all three gaps on this plan Jira, custom reporting, and advanced automation are all included — no upgrade needed, unlike both Help Scout and Freshdesk.
Best multi-brand setup Each OTT platform gets its own department with a separate email identity, branded templates, widget, and SLA — all from one shared workspace.
Blueprint automation for OTT flows Visual workflow builder enforces consistent handling of access issues, PPV disputes, and refunds across all 7 brands — fewer errors, easier to train new agents.
Slightly cheaper than current $115/mo vs $125/mo — and that gap widens if we are currently paying extra inbox add-on fees on Help Scout (+$10/mo per inbox).
Freshdesk Growth ($75/mo) is a valid alternative if cost is the priority — it fixes Jira and saves $600/yr. The tradeoff: custom reporting is locked behind the Pro plan ($245/mo), and the multi-brand setup is less structured than Zoho's department system.
