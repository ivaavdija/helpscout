Two things to keep in mind
Inquiry ≠ dispute
An inquiry is a question before money moves - answer it and it closes with no fee and no hit to our rate. A dispute means the funds are already pulled.
The window is short
Miss the 7-21 day deadline and the dispute is lost automatically - responding late is the most common way we lose. Evidence can be submitted only once.
